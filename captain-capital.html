01The problem

Why this market is hard

1

No domain history

Cold infrastructure into SMB data means the first four weeks decide whether the account has a future at all.

2

The category’s own words trigger deletion

“Funding”, “approved”, “pre-qualified”, a dollar figure in the subject — all of it reads as spam before the first sentence.

3

SMB data decays fast

Heavy spam-trap density and high list decay punish a new sender harder than an established one.

02The approach

What we built

01

Warmed domains in sequence

Multiple domains brought online in stages with per-domain volume held deliberately low while reputation established. 4.94% bounce across the first 36,873 sends.

02

Wrote questions, not offers

“Worth a look”. “Does this apply?” In a category where everyone leads with a number, restraint earns the open.

03

Ran the structural A/B at real volume

Business name vs no name, split properly — to settle whether personalising helps or hurts in a market trained to spot a merge.

04

Qualified in the inbox

Replies were triaged and labelled on arrival, so the client worked a queue of live funding requests rather than a raw inbox.
