01The problem

Why this market is hard

1

Budgets are grant-dependent

Many districts cannot buy until they win a COPS grant. The conversation is about the application, not the product.

2

Authority is invisible

Safety sits with IT, Facilities or an Emergency Management division — different in every county, and unknowable from outside.

3

The subject is grave

This is school shootings. Get the tone wrong and there is no second chance, with that district or its neighbours.

02The approach

What we built

01

Led with the grant, not the product

Campaigns were timed to COPS cycles and fall drill scheduling — the two moments a district can actually act.

02

Role-level tracks across 146 live campaigns

A superintendent worries about district liability and grant sequencing. A safety director worries whether the drill works.

03

Opened on the operational gap

A panic button that only alerts dispatch leaves the people down the hall with no instruction. Naming that produced replies from emergency managers.

04

Named the district next door

Where a neighbouring district already ran the platform, we said so. Administrators trust peers over vendors.
