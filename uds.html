01The problem

Why this market is hard

1

No intent data exists

A fire department has no funding round, no hiring signal, no tech stack to enrich against. Nothing to trigger on.

2

The buyer is hidden

The decision often sits with a wellness coordinator or union local two rungs from the person on your list.

3

Filters treat you as hostile

Several replies arrived with the city’s own external-sender warning still attached to them.

02The approach

What we built

01

Split by role, not by org

Fire, police, sheriff, EMS, municipal HR and union locals each got their own track. A chief’s reason to act is cancer exposure; a city manager’s is liability.

02

Led with peer proof

One state police force opened a 500-officer cardiac pilot and 711 volunteered. That answered the turnout objection before it was raised.

03

Built for .gov filters

Distributed domains, conservative per-domain volume. 2.36% bounce across 286,963 sends.

04

Wrote emails to be forwarded

Short and self-explanatory to a third reader, because the first recipient usually isn’t the buyer.
