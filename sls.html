01The problem

Why this market is hard

1

They get this every day

Directors of listed companies receive capital-markets outreach constantly and have seen every version of it.

2

The topic is sensitive

A director selling stock triggers a public disclosure notice with their name on it. That is the problem — and the reason care matters.

3

The list cannot be bought

Shareholding structures, escrow conditions, liquidity and exchange rules all differ. Get the security wrong and it is dead on arrival.

02The approach

What we built

01

Segmented by exchange, not size

ASX, AIM, LSE, NASDAQ, NYSE, OTC and NSX each run under different rules, framing and objections. Company size was irrelevant.

02

Personalised at security level

Every message was built around the recipient’s own listed holding. The exact construction is the client’s edge and is withheld here.

03

Named the obstacle first

Approved-securities lists, disclosure on director sales, daily traded volume. For this buyer, being understood beats being flattered.

04

Killed losers fast

Four of 21 campaigns never earned their send volume and were stopped inside the first weeks.
