01The problem

Why this market is hard

1

Owners get pitched daily

One reply put it exactly: “Can you actually do it, or are you just going to switch it up and offer me some MCA that will make things worse?”

2

Interest is not the problem

Every SMB owner wants capital. Belief is the problem — the category spent its credibility years ago.

3

Volume alone makes it worse

More of the wrong message into a cynical market just accelerates domain burn.

02The approach

What we built

01

A/B’d the greeting at full scale

Business vs personal, with name vs without — four families at real volume, not a token test. The data picked the winner and it carried the rest of the engagement.

02

Nine versions in twelve weeks

V1 through V9, each built on the previous version’s reply data. Underperformers were paused, not left running.

03

Under-promised in the subject line

“Might be useful”. “Worth a look”. Restraint beat every version that led with a dollar figure.

04

Built infrastructure for the volume

266,234 sends across three months at 4.23% bounce — which is why month three performed like month one.
